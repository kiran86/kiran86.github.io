Atlas Labs’ HR dataset was imported into Tableau, where exploratory analysis revealed key attrition factors such as departmental turnover, tenure, and satisfaction scores. Visualizations showed higher attrition in certain roles, prompting targeted retention strategies to address underlying issues and improve employee retention
