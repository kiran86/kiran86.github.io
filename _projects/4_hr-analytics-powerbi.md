Atlas Labs’ HR dataset was imported into Power BI, where exploratory analysis and DAX formulas revealed key attrition factors such as departmental turnover, tenure, and satisfaction scores. Visualizations showed higher attrition in certain roles, prompting targeted retention strategies to address underlying issues and improve employee retention
